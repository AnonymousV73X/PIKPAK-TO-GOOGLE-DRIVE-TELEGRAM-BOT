What's about to happen

rclone is the free, open-source engine that actually moves your files between PikPak and your destination. Relay installs it for you, keeps your login details on this machine only, and gives you a dashboard on top of it.

Connect PikPak

Generate a login block in your terminal, then paste it below.

1

Open a terminal — rclone's already installed from Step 2.

2

Run this: rclone config⧉ then type n⧉ for "New remote":

3

Name it exactly this (must match, all caps):

PIKKY⧉
4

Storage type — rclone jumps to the right number for you:

pikpak⧉
5

For user, enter your PikPak email. For pass, type y⧉ to obscure it, then enter your PikPak password.

6

Press Enter through any other prompts. At the end, confirm with y⧉ then quit with q⧉.

7

Open rclone.conf:

Windows (CMD): notepad %APPDATA%\rclone\rclone.conf⧉ Windows (PowerShell): notepad $env:APPDATA\rclone\rclone.conf⧉ macOS/Linux: ~/.config/rclone/rclone.conf⧉

Note: %APPDATA% only expands in CMD. In PowerShell use $env:APPDATA instead, or the path will fail to resolve.

8

Copy the whole [PIKKY] block (that line down to the next blank line) and paste it below.

rclone PikPak docs ↗

Choose your destination

Google Drive

Best for most people. Uses your existing Google account storage.

WebDAV

Free, works with Nextcloud, Koofr, and other WebDAV-compatible storage.

1

Open a terminal — rclone's already installed from Step 2.

2

Run this: rclone config⧉ then type n⧉ for "New remote":

3

Name it exactly this (must match, all caps):

GDRIVE⧉
4

Storage type — rclone jumps to the right number for you:

drive⧉
5

Leave client_id and client_secret blank (press Enter) unless you have your own Google API credentials.

6

For scope, choose 1⧉ (full access). Leave root_folder_id and service_account_file blank, then type n⧉ for "Edit advanced config".

7

Type y⧉ to use auto config — this opens your browser to sign in and authorize Google Drive access.

8

Type n⧉ for "Configure as a Shared Drive". Press Enter through any other prompts. At the end, confirm with y⧉ then quit with q⧉.

9

Open rclone.conf:

Windows (CMD): notepad %APPDATA%\rclone\rclone.conf⧉ Windows (PowerShell): notepad $env:APPDATA\rclone\rclone.conf⧉ macOS/Linux: ~/.config/rclone/rclone.conf⧉
10

Copy the whole [GDRIVE] block (that line down to the next blank line) and paste it below.

rclone Google Drive docs ↗
